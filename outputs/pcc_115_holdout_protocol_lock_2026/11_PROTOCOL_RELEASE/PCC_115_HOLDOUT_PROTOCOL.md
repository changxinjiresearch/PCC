# PCC 115 holdout protocol lock

This is a prelocked independent-validation protocol. The 115-patient cohort is deterministic and disjoint from the frozen 40. Stage A and Stage B require separate later authorizations. No 115-person P0, method execution, performance result, or LUMIERE execution is included.

The primary endpoint is patient-level Dice at fixed threshold 0.5. Full PCC vs Fixed and No-smoothing vs Full PCC are the two confirmatory comparisons with paired patient-level Wilcoxon tests and Holm adjustment.
