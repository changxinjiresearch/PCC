# Qualitative Figure Captions

## Layer 2 panels

Each nine-panel figure shows current T1c, current mask, future mask, clean future-change target, frozen held-out P0, PCC P1, PCC P10, P10−P0 correction, and the applicable guidance map. Cases were selected automatically using the prespecified rankings in `QUALITATIVE_PANEL_SOURCE_MAP.csv`: median, maximum, and minimum Layer 2 gain; smallest target; most fragmented target; and worst imperfect-guidance retention. Axial slices maximize the locked target/correction score. No case was selected by visual appearance.

## Layer 1 panels

The two Layer 1 Formal v1 panels show the global maximum-improvement and maximum-decline cases under the locked case-level Dice ranking. Each shows current T1c, target mask, baseline and PCC probabilities, their difference, and thresholded outputs. The saved source map records fold, checkpoints, thresholded result provenance, and confirms that no training was performed for figure creation.

These figures are qualitative illustrations of locked quantitative selections and do not replace case-level statistical analysis.
