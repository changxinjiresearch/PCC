# Scientific interpretation boundaries

- These analyses are retrospective and target-conditioned. They do not demonstrate deployment-time future prediction after the held-out P0 stage.
- PCC updates a probability map through iterative error-guided refinement; it does not update predictor parameters.
- EIA blends are oracle-like retrospective controls with access to the same target. PCC must not be described as superior when an EIA blend has the higher result.
- New 2026 ablations, target derangement, degraded guidance, target definitions, and difference-map control are prespecified new analyses, not historical reproductions.
- Repeated seeds and shift directions are technical repeats within a case, not independent samples.
- Forty internal cases limit precision and generalizability.
- Layer 3 supports pathology-related spatial reliance and correction localization, not molecular, cellular, causal, or mechanistic pathology claims.
- No LUMIERE or other external experiment is part of this internal-completion stage.
